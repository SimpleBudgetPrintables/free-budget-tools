# Free Budget Tools

Free budgeting tools from [Simple Budget Printables](https://simplebudgetprintables.com) — try them all at [simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/).

Every tool is free, runs in the browser, needs no signup, and uploads nothing.

## The tools

- [Leftover Check](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Run one paycheck through the Leftover, On Purpose method and see your leftover before you spend it.
- [Debt Payoff Visualizer](https://sbp-debt-payoff-visualizer.netlify.app/) — Enter your debts, drag the extra-per-month slider, and watch your debt-free date slide closer.
- [Paycheck Splitter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Type in your take-home pay and watch the check slice itself into bills, savings and spending.
- [Months-To-Go Counter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Name your goal, what you have saved, and what you can put away each month, and see how many months to go.
- [Extra-Check Finder](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Paid every two weeks? Enter one payday and find your two third-paycheck months.
- [Couples Merger](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Merge two paychecks and every bill into one household picture.
- [Sinking Funds Counter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Four jars for coming costs — Christmas, car repairs, vacation, home repairs.
- [Subscription Counter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Line up your subscriptions and see the monthly total and the yearly total.
- [Bill Calendar Splitter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Your month on one calendar: two checks, every bill on its due day, each bill claimed by the check that covers it.
- [Savings Challenge Picker](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Four savings challenges: the 52-Week Classic, the Reverse, the 26-Check, and Round-Up Fifty.
- [Grocery Over/Under Check](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Set the month's grocery budget and every week gets a verdict in dollars.
- [No-Spend Streak Counter](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Thirty squares, one tap per day: count your current streak, your longest streak, and your no-spend days.
- [Irregular-Income Floor Builder](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — Six months of uneven income become six bars with one floor line: the steady number you budget from.
- [Short-Check Helper](https://simplebudgetprintables.com/free-tools/) — When a check lands smaller than usual, see the gap and trim it in a calm order.
